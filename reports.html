Reports

Reference for fleet visibility — report types, alerts, scheduling, exports and history retention. Every report starts from live inventory and ends as proof: for audits, for SLAs, for Monday morning.

REFERENCE

Report types

Five ready-made lenses — inventory, compliance, apps, OTA and usage — exportable for records and proof.

2

Standard reports are the five ready-made lenses on a fleet. Each one filters by group, zone, status or time range, so the same report serves a technician checking ten units and an auditor reviewing ten thousand.

ReportShowsAnswers
InventoryHardware and software per device: model, OS, apps, memory, ownershipWhat do we own, and how old is it?
CompliancePolicy adherence and violations fleet-wide, with per-device drill-downWhich units break the rules?
AppInstall state and versions, pivoted by device or by appDid the rollout actually land?
OTAFirmware rollout stages: downloaded, installing, failed, rolled backWhere is the update stuck?
Data usage / SLAConsumption per device and signage uptime percentagesCan we prove the SLA?
Where to find itReports → pick type → filter → Export (CSV/Excel/PDF)
Alerts

Alerts watch thresholds so humans don’t have to. Instead of patrolling dashboards, admins define what counts as trouble once — then get told the moment it happens, on the channel that reaches them.

TriggerExample
OfflineDevice silent for longer than the grace period
Geofence exitDelivery unit leaves its assigned zone
Low batteryKiosk drops below the charge threshold mid-shift
Non-complianceUnit drifts out of its assigned policies
ChannelsEmail for records, SMS for urgency, webhook for piping into ticketing or dashboards.
AssignmentRules attach to zones or groups, so store managers hear about their stores — not the whole fleet.
Where to find itAlerts → Rule → Channel → Assign
Scheduled reports

Scheduled reports run themselves: pick a report, its filters and recipients once, and it arrives by email on cadence — daily health digests, weekly compliance packs, monthly SLA evidence. Automation replaces the Monday-morning export ritual and guarantees the audit trail exists before anyone asks for it.

Where to find itReports → pick type → Schedule → recipients → cadence
Export formats

Every report exports three ways. CSV feeds spreadsheets and scripts, Excel arrives formatted for sharing with non-technical readers, and PDF freezes a timestamped snapshot for compliance files. Exports honor the active filters, so what you see is what leaves the building.

CONCEPT

History & scheduling

Retention sets how far back you can investigate; scheduled delivery brings reports to inboxes automatically.

1
Retention & delivery

Retention decides how far back you can investigate; delivery decides how reports reach you. Short history with manual exports suits daily ops and keeps storage lean. Standard plans keep 60-day device history; extended plans add unlimited history, scheduled email and custom BI export for ERP and analytics tooling.
