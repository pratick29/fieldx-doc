RESTRICTION CATEGORY

Reboot Device

Scheduled reboots on daily or multi-day cadences, landing within about 30 minutes of the chosen time. Night windows keep signage fresh.

2
What it doesSets how frequently the device reboots on a recurring schedule.
OptionWhat it does
NeverThe device is not rebooted on a schedule.
EverydayThe device reboots once every day.
Every Alternate DayThe device reboots every other day.
Every 3 DaysThe device reboots every 3 days.
Every 4 DaysThe device reboots every 4 days.
Every 5 DaysThe device reboots every 5 days.
Every 6 DaysThe device reboots every 6 days.
Available forFieldX EMM
Where to find itRestrictions / Reboot Device / Reboot Schedule

When should I use this? Use this to clear memory leaks or stuck states on always-on field devices, such as kiosks or digital signage.

What it doesSets the approximate time of day the scheduled reboot occurs, within roughly a 30-minute window.
What to enterEnter the target reboot time.
Available forFieldX EMM
Where to find itRestrictions / Reboot Device / Reboot around time

When should I use this? Use this to schedule reboots during off-hours so they don't interrupt active use.
