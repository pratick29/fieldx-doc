Enroll Device

Set up and enrol an Android device with FieldX using QR code, Web Installer, or the FieldX Installer app.

Android Step-by-step guide FieldX EMM
Before you begin Choose the enrolment method supported by your device. Some methods require a new or factory-reset device, while others require USB or wireless debugging.

Questions?Contact info@fieldx.io or visit support.fieldx.io.

A browser-driven flow for devices without zero-touch or QR support: USB cable, Chrome and USB debugging carry the agent over. Built for distributed and franchise deployments.

App Based Enrolment 6

No computer needed: one Android phone enrols another over wireless ADB. Same Wi-Fi, wireless debugging on, and the installer app walks from pairing to setup.

Manual enrolment (ADB) 3

The hands-on path for devices without QR provisioning: nine ADB commands that install the agent, grant its privileges and configure it. Needs command-line comfort; script it for batches.
