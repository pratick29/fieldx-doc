App management

Control exactly which apps exist and run on each device.

What it doesDeploys, updates or removes apps remotely with no user tap, including version pinning and staged rollout.
Where to find itApps → Add (upload APK or Play link) → Assign group → Silent mode
What it doesHide or suspend distracting apps; protect required apps from uninstall; or lock the device to an approved allowlist where anything else is hidden, disabled or uninstalled.

Full option tables live in Restrictions → App Management & Control

What it doesA curated store with only approved productivity tools — no Google Play dependency; brandable on Enterprise.

When should I use this? Fleets that must never touch public stores.
