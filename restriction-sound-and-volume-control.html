RESTRICTION CATEGORY

Sound & Volume Control

Control audio settings and volume. Some entries below (ringer mode, and the finer per-setting controls) are recorded in the FieldX source with minimal detail; where that is the case, this guide says so rather than guessing at behavior.

6
What it doesControls whether the user can use the volume controls.
OptionWhat it does
AllowAllows the behavior controlled by this restriction.
DisallowPrevents the behavior controlled by this restriction.
Available forFieldX EMM, Android Enterprise
Where to find itRestrictions / Sound & Volume Control / Allow Adjust Volume

When should I use this? Use this to lock volume at a fixed level, for example on shared devices in a quiet environment.

What it doesSets the device's overall system volume level across all audio streams.
OptionWhat it does
Minimum Volume (all streams)Sets every audio stream to its minimum volume.
Medium Volume (all streams)Sets every audio stream to a medium volume.
Maximum Volume (all streams)Sets every audio stream to its maximum volume.
Available forNot specified in the FieldX source.
Where to find itRestrictions / Sound & Volume Control / Set System Volume

When should I use this? Use this to standardize the starting volume level across a device fleet.

What it doesControls the device's default ringer mode for calls and notifications.
OptionWhat it does
Silent Ringer & NotificationsRinger and notification sounds are silenced.
Vibrate Ringer & NotificationsRinger and notifications vibrate instead of sounding.
Available forNot specified in the FieldX source.
Where to find itRestrictions / Sound & Volume Control / Ringer Mode (Silent / Vibrate)

When should I use this? Use this in environments where audible alerts are disruptive, such as clinical or classroom settings.

Important notes

Note: The FieldX source lists Silent and Vibrate as two separate settings rather than as options of one control; this guide groups them here for readability since they govern the same behavior. Confirm the exact configuration path in your FieldX console.

What it doesControls the user's general ability to adjust sound settings on the device.

Configured through its own settings window in the FieldX console rather than a simple Allow/Disallow toggle.

Available forNot specified in the FieldX source.
Where to find itRestrictions / Sound & Volume Control / Allow user control sound

When should I use this? Use this when a broader lock on sound settings is needed beyond the specific volume and ringer controls above.

Important notes

Note: The FieldX source does not provide further detail on this setting beyond its name.
