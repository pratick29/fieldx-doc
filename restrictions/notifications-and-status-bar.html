Notifications & Status Bar

Hide distracting notifications (needs the listener) or force all of them on for alerting fleets.

2
What it doesHides notifications from selected apps. Requires the user to enable Notification Listener access for FieldX.

Configured through its own settings window in the FieldX console rather than a simple Allow/Disallow toggle.

Available forNot specified in the FieldX source.
Where to find itRestrictions / Notifications & Status Bar / Hide Notifications

When should I use this? Use this to suppress distracting or sensitive notifications on managed devices.

Important notes

Note: Requires Notification Listener to be enabled by the user before it can take effect.

What it doesAllows or disallows notifications from all apps on the device status bar. Available only on Samsung devices.
OptionWhat it does
AllowAllows the behavior controlled by this restriction.
DisallowPrevents the behavior controlled by this restriction.
Available forSamsung & FieldX EMM
Where to find itRestrictions / Notifications & Status Bar / Show Notifications (Samsung Only)

When should I use this? Use this on Samsung fleets where notification visibility should be controlled fleet-wide rather than per app.
