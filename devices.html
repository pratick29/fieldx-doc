Inventory & details

Find any device and drill into its live state.

What it doesLists every enrolled device with search and filters — by group, zone, status, model, OS, compliance and enrollment recency.
Where to find itDevices / Inventory

When should I use this? Daily fleet checks, auditing a rollout, or locating a specific unit by IMEI/serial.

What it doesShows 100+ live parameters per device: battery, storage, RAM, CPU, temperature, signal, data usage, GPS, uptime, app crashes, policy compliance, network status.

When should I use this? Diagnosing a single misbehaving device before taking action.
