SETUP

Account & console

Sign up and learn your way around the FieldX Console — the portal where every fleet action happens.

Your account lives on the FieldX console — the web portal where every fleet action happens. Sign up, verify, log in: the dashboard you land on is the same surface you will operate daily.

1. Go to admin.fieldx.io/accounts/register → Sign Up.
2. Verify email → Login at admin.fieldx.io/users/login.
3. You land on the FieldX Console (dashboard with fleet health, compliance, zones).

The console mirrors the fleet’s structure: health up top, inventory and grouping in the middle, policies-apps-kiosk-OTA as the levers, reports and downloads as the outputs. Learn this map once and every guide below makes sense.

SETUP

First device & requirements

Enroll one device in about three minutes to prove the pipeline, then check platform coverage for the rest of the fleet.

The first device proves the whole pipeline — console entry, QR code, agent install, policy download — in about three minutes. Everything after it is the same flow at scale.

1. Console → Devices → Add → QR Enrollment → download/print QR.
2. Factory-reset Android → tap 6x on welcome → scan QR.
3. Device auto-downloads agent + policies + apps. Done in ~3 min.

Need another way to enroll? See Enroll Device →

FieldX is built for constrained environments: decade-old Android versions, de-Googled builds, offline sites with SMS fallback. If the hardware runs Android 5 or better and you have a browser, you can start.

PlatformsAndroid 5–15 (stock, Knox, or custom AOSP), select Linux builds, Apple (iOS/iPadOS/macOS).
GoogleWorks without Google Play / Firebase — ideal for custom builds & restricted regions.
ConnectivityInternet preferred, but encrypted SMS fallback keeps control offline.
BrowserChrome / Edge / Firefox / Safari. Private-cloud / on-premise available on request.

One enrolled device turns reading into operating. Bulk enrollment repeats the QR flow across hundreds of units; the Devices inventory is where you will spend most days.
