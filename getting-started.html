Getting Started

Create your account, tour the console, and enroll your first device in about 15 minutes.

15-minute setupQR enrollmentStep-by-step

Start hereFollow the steps below in order: account → console tour → first device. Then continue with Enroll Device →
SETUP

Account & console

Sign up and learn your way around the FieldX Console.

1. Go to admin.fieldx.io/accounts/register → Sign Up.
2. Verify email → Login at admin.fieldx.io/users/login.
3. You land on the FieldX Console (dashboard with fleet health, compliance, zones).
AreaUse
DashboardLive fleet status, online/offline, compliance donut, recent alerts
Devices / InventorySearch, filter, drill into 100+ params per device

SETUP

First device & requirements

Enroll one device fast, then check what else is supported.

1. Console → Devices → Add → QR Enrollment → download/print QR.
2. Factory-reset Android → tap 6x on welcome → scan QR.
3. Device auto-downloads agent + policies + apps. Done in ~3 min.

Need another way to enroll? See Enroll Device →

PlatformsAndroid 5–15 (stock, Knox, or custom AOSP), select Linux builds, Apple (iOS/iPadOS/macOS).
GoogleWorks without Google Play / Firebase — ideal for custom builds & restricted regions.
ConnectivityInternet preferred, but encrypted SMS fallback keeps control offline.
BrowserChrome / Edge / Firefox / Safari. Private-cloud / on-premise available on request.
