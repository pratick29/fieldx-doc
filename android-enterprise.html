FieldX Documentation

Android Enterprise

Google's enterprise framework inside FieldX — work profiles for personal devices, full management for corporate units, and Managed Play for apps. Orange pills mark every restriction that needs it.

REFERENCE

Management with AE

Two depths of control from the same framework — pick per device, not per fleet.

2
Work profile (BYOD)

Profile-owner mode manages only the work profile and leaves the personal side alone — company apps and data stay controlled while photos and messages stay private. The BYOD compromise, backed by Android Enterprise.

Fully managed (corporate)

Device-owner mode — usually via QR provisioning — grants full-device control including wipe and kiosk. A managed device shows its AE roots in the inventory: the EMM agent fields record the installation type.

Where to find itDevices → click a Device ID → Network & EMM
REFERENCE

Play and inventory

Approved apps and a synced device list — the two AE console jobs.

2
Managed Google Play

Alongside silent pushes and the private store, apps can be approved from Managed Google Play — Google's enterprise channel, curated by the admin. See the deployment paths in Apps →

Sync AE devices

Google-side changes don't walk over by themselves: the Sync AE Devices toolbar action pulls the latest Android Enterprise inventory into FieldX. Run it after adding devices on the Google side.

Where to find itDevices → inventory toolbar → Sync AE Devices

Reading the pills

Every restriction carries platform pills: green is FieldX EMM, orange is Android Enterprise, blue is Samsung. Some entries carry a version floor — the default-apps family, for example, needs Android Enterprise v16+.

AE-only entries

A few restrictions exist only on Android Enterprise — the credential-provider policies, for instance. The fastest way to find them all is the platform column of the master list.
